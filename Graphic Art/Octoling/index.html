Octoling

This is an animation I made of an octoling from my favorite videogame series Splatoon! I made it in blender for my Graphic Art class, and it ended up being displayed at our school's Celebration of the Arts! I learned a lot about blender as a program, and am definitely going to be using it more in the future. In the end, this was displayed on a slanted piece of acrylic in a black box, giving it a hologram-like effect (this is why the backround is black). Overall, I think it turned out really well and I learned a lot about 3d modeling organic shapes through the process of making it!
